Questions

Good to know.

Straight answers before you message. Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

Do I need a script or existing footage?

No. A short note about your offer is enough to start. I can shape the script and visuals from your brief, or work from your draft if you have one.

What affects turnaround time?

Length, number of scenes, revisions and how fast feedback arrives. We agree a clear timeline with your quote before production.

Can you use my brand colours, logo and product images?

Yes. Send your logo, colours, fonts and product photos or links. They guide the look so the final film feels truly yours.

What about commercial usage, music and asset licensing?

Usage, platforms and duration are agreed in writing before work starts. Music and stock assets are licensed for your agreed use, and sources are shared on request.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I am based in Akure and create for brands worldwide. We communicate on WhatsApp and email, with files delivered online.

How do revisions work?

Each package includes an agreed number of revision rounds. Change requests are grouped per round so we stay fast and focused.
